SEC.02 — THE PROBLEM

Africa's industrial
potential is untapped.

A

Fragmented services

A founder with a product idea today has to separately find a designer, an engineer, a factory, a supplier, and a course — each in a different place, in a different language of trust. AMEP puts them behind one door.

B

Missing infrastructure knowledge

Building a factory is treated as a black box. AMEP publishes the actual technicalities — site design, equipment selection, licensing — as a service you can commission, not a secret you have to discover.

C

No shared floor

Makers across the continent rarely meet each other. The Community page is a standing invitation into Built by Africa: Makers & Manufacturers — open, free, no vetting.
